# REQ-004.002 Change Decision Status

## User Story

As a project decision-maker, I want to accept, reject, deprecate, supersede, or archive a decision so that its current standing is explicit and retired decisions leave the working set.

## Group

REQ-004 Decision Records

## Criticality

Must have

## Description

A decision status moves only through the status verbs of its own command family, `papyrus <slug> <verb>`; no edit option sets it (REQ-004.001). The status vocabulary is fixed, shared by every decision type, and not configurable per type: `proposed`, `accepted`, `rejected`, `deprecated`, `superseded`. A new decision starts `proposed`.

**Status transitions.** Each status verb performs exactly one transition and is rejected with a validation outcome, writing nothing, when the decision is not in the required starting status:

- `papyrus <slug> accept <ID>` — `proposed` to `accepted`.
- `papyrus <slug> reject <ID>` — `proposed` to `rejected`.
- `papyrus <slug> deprecate <ID>` — `accepted` to `deprecated`.
- `papyrus <slug> supersede <ID> --by <SUCCESSOR-ID>` — `accepted` to `superseded`.

A decision cannot be accepted or rejected twice, and a rejected decision cannot be accepted or deprecated.

**Supersession.** A decision may be superseded by exactly one decision of the same type. `supersede` records the successor identifier in the front-matter `superseded_by` key and sets the status to `superseded` (REQ-005.010). A successor of a different type, an unknown successor, or a second supersession is rejected and writes nothing.

**Archive.** `papyrus <slug> archive <ID>` moves the record's file into the `archive/` subfolder of the folder that holds it — the main decision folder when one type is configured, or the type's subfolder when several are (REQ-004.001). Archiving moves the file and changes no status: a `deprecated` decision stays `deprecated`, a `rejected` decision stays `rejected`, and a `superseded` decision stays `superseded`. The moved record keeps its identifier. Archiving is allowed only when the status is `rejected`, `superseded`, or `deprecated`; it is rejected for `proposed` and `accepted`. Archived decisions are excluded from the list; search returns them only with `--include-archive` (REQ-009.001); they remain viewable, and an edit is rejected with a validation outcome and writes nothing. No command returns them to the decision folder: there is no restore command.

**Frozen content.** Once a decision has left `proposed`, its content cannot be edited or deleted (REQ-004.001); only the status verbs and the archive command apply.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A decision status moves only through the status verbs, and no edit option sets it.
- The status vocabulary is exactly `proposed`, `accepted`, `rejected`, `deprecated`, and `superseded` for every decision type, and is not configurable.
- `accept` moves `proposed` to `accepted`, and `reject` moves `proposed` to `rejected`.
- `deprecate` moves `accepted` to `deprecated`, and `supersede` moves `accepted` to `superseded`.
- A status verb applied from a status other than its required starting status is rejected with a validation outcome and writes nothing.
- `supersede <ID> --by <SUCCESSOR-ID>` records the successor in the front-matter `superseded_by` key and sets the status to `superseded`.
- A successor of a different type, an unknown successor, or a second supersession is rejected and writes nothing.
- A decision is superseded by at most one successor and only by a decision of the same type.
- `archive <ID>` moves the record's file into the `archive/` subfolder of the folder that holds it and keeps its identifier.
- Archiving changes no status: a `deprecated`, `rejected`, or `superseded` decision keeps that status after the move.
- Archiving a `proposed` or `accepted` decision is rejected with a validation outcome and writes nothing.
- Archived decisions are excluded from the list; they are returned by search only with `--include-archive`, remain viewable, and are not editable.
- No command returns an archived decision to the decision folder; no restore command exists.
- A decision that has left `proposed` cannot be edited or deleted.
