# REQ-004.001 Manage Decisions

## User Story

As a project decision-maker, I want to record a decision and revise it while it is still being formed so that the record captured is the one that was settled.

## Group

REQ-004 Decision Records

## Criticality

Must have

## Description

**Create.** `papyrus decision create <TITLE>` creates a decision from the Nygard template: a title, a decision type, the decision text, the context, and the consequences. The decision text, the context, and the consequences are multiline and accept `-` to read the value from standard input. Labels and references are optional general metadata, and a reference may name any managed record, including business and non-functional requirements. A new decision starts `proposed`.

**Type and identifier.** The decision type defaults to `adr`. The built-in types are `adr` and `bdr`, and further types may be declared in the configuration. A decision identifier uses the type's configured prefix and the shared `id_padding` (REQ-001.005), and the type is derived from that prefix.

**Update.** `papyrus decision edit <ID>` changes the title, the decision text, the context, the consequences, the labels, or the references. An edit is allowed only while the decision status is `proposed`; once the decision has left `proposed`, its content, its title, its labels, and its references are frozen and an edit is rejected with a validation outcome and writes nothing. Status changes are not part of an edit; they belong to REQ-004.002.

**Delete.** `papyrus decision delete <ID>` permanently removes a decision, and is allowed only while the status is `proposed`. Deletion asks for confirmation, and `--force` skips the prompt. A decision that has left `proposed` cannot be deleted; it is archived instead (REQ-004.002).

**Status storage.** The decision status stays in the `## Status` section of the document body (REQ-005.010).

## Acceptance Criteria

- `papyrus decision create <TITLE>` creates a decision with a title, a type, a decision text, a context, and consequences.
- The decision text, context, and consequences accept `-` to read the value from standard input.
- The decision type defaults to `adr`; `adr` and `bdr` are built in, and further types may be declared in the configuration.
- A decision identifier uses its type's configured prefix and `id_padding`, and the type is derived from the prefix.
- A decision may reference any managed record, including business and non-functional requirements, and may carry labels.
- A new decision starts `proposed`.
- `papyrus decision edit <ID>` changes title, decision text, context, consequences, labels, or references while the status is `proposed`.
- An edit of a decision whose status is not `proposed` is rejected with a validation outcome and writes nothing.
- An edit never changes the status.
- `papyrus decision delete <ID>` permanently removes a decision while its status is `proposed`, asks for confirmation, and `--force` skips the prompt.
- A decision whose status is not `proposed` cannot be deleted.
- The decision status is stored in the `## Status` body section.
