## Scenario: Supersede archives the old mockup and records its successor

GIVEN two mockups MOCK-001 and MOCK-002, both pending
WHEN a contributor runs supersede for MOCK-001 with MOCK-002
THEN MOCK-001 is archived and its metadata records MOCK-002 as its successor
  AND MOCK-002 keeps its state and metadata
  AND a later state transition on MOCK-001 is rejected
