# REQ-004 Decision Records

## User Story
As a project decision-maker, I want to record, revise, settle, and retire decisions so that their history and current standing stay clear.

## Description
This group covers the decision artifact category (REQ-007.001): decision records, their types and content, status changes, supersession, and archiving. Records follow the Nygard ADR template and the fixed Nygard status vocabulary. Content is editable and deletable only while a decision is proposed; status moves only through its verbs. Archiving moves the record into the decision folder's archived/ subfolder and leaves its status unchanged (REQ-004.002).
