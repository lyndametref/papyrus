# GLO-014 Record Category

## Definition
One of the three fixed lifecycle groups Papyrus manages: backlog, decision, and document. A record category groups record types and owns their lifecycle rules; it is not a record and has no identifier or body of its own. Configuration cannot add, remove, or rename a record category.

Related terms: Record, Record Type, Document Type, Decision Type.
