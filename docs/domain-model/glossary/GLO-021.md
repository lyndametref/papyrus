# GLO-021 Member Record

## Definition
A record that belongs to a group in a GroupedMarkdown document type: it has a dotted identifier of the form <PREFIX>-<group-sequence>.<member-sequence>, its own field sequence, its own status, and a group link to its group.

Related terms: Group Record, GroupedMarkdown, Role.
