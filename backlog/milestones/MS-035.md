## Description
Internal checkpoint of one deliverable: browser routes for document types only.

Goal: one descriptor-driven list, view, edit, conflict, and not-found surface replaces per-type branches, per ADR-D, ADR-0020, ADR-0028, ADR-0033, and ADR-0035. Record envelope carries a model-neutral field map; presentation facts come from the config seed.

Acceptance criteria:
AC-1 The 9 conflict-page renderers collapse to one generic conflict page and the 5 not-found pages to one generic 404.
AC-2 Document list, view, and edit pages render the same semantic structure as the MS-032 fixtures: same selectors, text, navigation, form fields, and status codes; byte-identical HTML is not required.
AC-3 GroupedMarkdown renders group and member records with role-aware field sets; SingleHtmlMockup renders its fixed pending/approved/archived metadata; SingleMarkdown renders its single field set.
AC-4 Presentation facts (labels, section headings, help text, field order) come from the config seed, not from per-type code.
AC-5 Reference visibility follows the seed reference_visibility per type and role, superseding ADR-0035's discriminator as amended.
AC-6 One generic form_post handler serves every document type and preserves optimistic concurrency behavior.
AC-7 No document-type dispatch remains in the browser layer; document slugs are never matched by name outside the seed and its tests.
AC-8 Backlog and decision browser surfaces are unchanged.
