# REQ-004.002 Change Decision Status

## User Story

As a project decision-maker, I want to accept, reject, deprecate, supersede, or archive a decision so that its current standing is explicit.

## Group

REQ-004 Decision Records

## Criticality

Must have

## Description

A decision status moves only through the status verbs; no edit option sets it (REQ-004.001). The status vocabulary is fixed and not configurable: `proposed`, `accepted`, `rejected`, `deprecated`, `superseded`, and `archived`. A new decision starts `proposed`.

**Verbs and transitions.** Each verb performs exactly one transition and is rejected with a validation outcome, writing nothing, when the decision is not in the required starting status:

- `papyrus decision accept <ID>` — `proposed` to `accepted`.
- `papyrus decision reject <ID>` — `proposed` to `rejected`.
- `papyrus decision deprecate <ID>` — `accepted` to `deprecated`.
- `papyrus decision supersede <ID> --by <SUCCESSOR-ID>` — `accepted` to `superseded`.
- `papyrus decision archive <ID>` — `rejected`, `superseded`, or `deprecated` to `archived`.

A decision that is `accepted` or `proposed` cannot be archived. A decision cannot be accepted or rejected twice, and a rejected decision cannot be accepted or deprecated.

**Supersession.** A decision may be superseded by exactly one decision of the same type. `supersede` records the successor identifier in the front-matter `superseded_by` key and sets the status to `superseded` (REQ-005.010). A successor of a different type, an unknown successor, or a second supersession is rejected and writes nothing.

**Archive.** A decision's `archived` is a status value, not a storage location: archiving sets the status, moves no file, and keeps the record in the decision folder. It is distinct from the document state `archived`, which moves a file into an `archived/` subfolder (REQ-005.007). An archived decision is excluded from the decision list (REQ-004.003).

**Frozen content.** Once a decision has left `proposed`, its content cannot be edited or deleted (REQ-004.001); only the status verbs above apply.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A decision status moves only through the status verbs, and no edit option sets it.
- The status vocabulary is exactly `proposed`, `accepted`, `rejected`, `deprecated`, `superseded`, and `archived`, and is not configurable.
- `accept` moves `proposed` to `accepted`, and `reject` moves `proposed` to `rejected`.
- `deprecate` moves `accepted` to `deprecated`, and `supersede` moves `accepted` to `superseded`.
- `archive` moves `rejected`, `superseded`, or `deprecated` to `archived`.
- Archiving an `accepted` or `proposed` decision is rejected with a validation outcome and writes nothing.
- A verb applied from a status other than its required starting status is rejected with a validation outcome and writes nothing.
- `supersede <ID> --by <SUCCESSOR-ID>` records the successor in the front-matter `superseded_by` key and sets the status to `superseded`.
- A successor of a different type, an unknown successor, or a second supersession is rejected and writes nothing.
- A decision is superseded by at most one successor and only by a decision of the same type.
- Archiving sets the status and moves no file, and the record stays in the decision folder.
- A decision's `archived` is distinct from the document state `archived`, which moves a file into an `archived/` subfolder.
- An archived decision is excluded from the decision list.
- A decision that has left `proposed` cannot be edited or deleted.
