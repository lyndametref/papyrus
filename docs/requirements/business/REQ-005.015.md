# REQ-005.015 Manage simple-record Records

## User Story

As a project contributor, I want to create, change, and retire the records of any simple-record document type so that documented content stays current and traceable.

## Group

REQ-005 Project Documentation Management

## Criticality

Must have

## Description

Every document type declared in the project configuration whose document model is `simple-record` is managed by this requirement. Such a type has one record per plain identifier (REQ-001.005), the record body is the type's field sequence, and the record carries the general front matter of REQ-005.010.

**Command family.** The verbs are `create`, `list`, `view`, `edit`, `status`, `archive`, and `delete`, derived from the document model (REQ-007.003) and addressed by the type's configured slug, which is also its command name.

**Title.** The title is the first positional `TITLE` on `create` and is also accepted as `--title` on `create` and `edit`. It is single-line, non-empty, required on create, and can never be cleared by an edit. Supplying both the positional `TITLE` and `--title` on one invocation is rejected and writes nothing. The title is not a field (REQ-005.010).

**Fields.** Each field of the type's field sequence contributes one option on the verbs the field declares, named by its `option` or by its `key` in kebab case when no option is declared (REQ-007.004, REQ-007.003). A required field must be supplied on create and may be changed but not cleared by an edit; an optional field may be supplied or cleared. A field of kind `list` accepts one value per occurrence, and repeated occurrences append list items. A field of kind `multiline` accepts `-` to read its value from standard input. `--editor` opens the record body in the configured editor. A create that omits a required field, and an edit that clears one, is rejected with one validation outcome and writes no file.

**General metadata.** `--label` and `--reference` are the general repeatable metadata options and are not fields.

**Status and lifecycle.** A record carries the front-matter `status` of its type. No create or edit option sets `status`; it changes only through the `status` and `archive` verbs (REQ-005.011). Archiving and deletion follow REQ-005.007.

**Viewing.** Listing and single-record view behaviour follows REQ-005.017.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The requirement applies to every configured document type whose document model is `simple-record`, and to no other type.
- A `simple-record` type has one record per plain identifier, and the record body is the type's field sequence.
- The command family is `create`, `list`, `view`, `edit`, `status`, `archive`, and `delete`, addressed by the type's configured slug.
- `create` takes the title as its first positional `TITLE`; `--title` is accepted on `create` and `edit`.
- A create without a title, with an empty title, with a multi-line title, or with both the positional `TITLE` and `--title` is rejected and writes no file; an edit cannot clear the title.
- Each declared field contributes one option on the verbs the field declares, named by its `option` or by its `key` in kebab case.
- A field whose verbs exclude `edit` has no edit option and is never writable after creation; a field whose verbs exclude `create` has no create option.
- A required field must be supplied on create and may be changed but not cleared by an edit; an optional field may be supplied or cleared.
- A create that omits a required field, and an edit that clears a required field, is rejected with one validation outcome and writes no file.
- A `list` field accepts one value per occurrence and appends list items; a `multiline` field accepts `-` to read its value from standard input.
- `--editor` edits the record body in the configured editor.
- `--label` and `--reference` are accepted as general repeatable metadata and are not fields.
- No create or edit option sets `status`; only the `status` and `archive` verbs write it.
- Archiving and deletion follow REQ-005.007; listing and viewing follow REQ-005.017.
