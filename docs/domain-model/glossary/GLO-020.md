# GLO-020 Group Record

## Definition
The record that heads a group in a GroupedMarkdown document type: it has a plain identifier, its own field sequence, and its own status, and its members are reached through the engine-owned group link. In the requirements context a group record is a requirement group.

Related terms: Member Record, GroupedMarkdown, Requirement Group, Role.
