# GLO-011 Group Record

## Definition
The record that heads a group in a Grouped Record document type: it has a plain identifier, its own field sequence, and its own status, and its members are reached through the engine-owned group link. In the requirements context a group record heads a group of requirements.

Related terms: Member Record, Grouped Record, Role.
