# REQ-005.001 Manage grouped-record Records

## User Story

As a project contributor, I want to create and maintain the group and member records of a grouped-record document type so that related content stays organised in one place.

## Group

REQ-005 Project Documentation Management

## Criticality

Must have

## Description

Every document type declared in the project configuration whose document model is `grouped-record` is managed by this requirement. Such a type has two roles (REQ-007.004): a group record with a plain identifier, and member records whose dotted identifier names their owning group's sequence (REQ-001.005). A member belongs to exactly one group, and a group holds only members of its own document type.

**Command family.** The verbs are `create-group`, `create`, `list`, `view`, `edit`, `status`, `archive`, and `delete`, derived from the document model (REQ-007.003) and addressed by the type's configured slug. The title contract, field options, required and clearing rules, `list` and `multiline` handling, and `--editor` of REQ-005.015 apply to both roles, each with its own field sequence.

**Group creation.** `papyrus <slug> create-group <TITLE>` creates a group record with the given title.

**Member creation.** `papyrus <slug> create <TITLE> --group <GROUP-ID>` creates a member and requires an existing active group of the same document type, supplied through the group slot or `--group`. An existing active group is reused, and a member create never creates a group. An unknown group, an archived group, or a group of another document type is rejected with one validation outcome and writes no file.

**Roles and fields.** A `grouped-record` type declares a group role and a member role, each with its own field sequence and independent order; a top-level field sequence on such a type is rejected (REQ-007.004). A record carries the fields of its own role.

**Group link.** The owning group is the reserved front-matter key `group`, written and maintained only by the engine. It is not a field, no option sets or changes it, and a member's group link cannot change after creation.

**Status and lifecycle.** Group and member records carry the front-matter `status` of their type; only the `status` and `archive` verbs write it (REQ-005.011). Archiving or deleting a group record changes no member's file, status, or identifier, and each member is archived or deleted only by a command that names that member (REQ-005.007).

**Viewing.** A list shows group records and their members merged in group order, then member order within a group, with member rows read-only; single-record view follows REQ-005.017.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The requirement applies to every configured document type whose document model is `grouped-record`, and to no other type.
- A group record has a plain identifier; a member record has a dotted identifier naming its group's sequence.
- The command family is `create-group`, `create`, `list`, `view`, `edit`, `status`, `archive`, and `delete`, addressed by the type's configured slug.
- `create-group <TITLE>` creates a group record with the given title.
- `create <TITLE> --group <GROUP-ID>` creates a member only when the group exists, is active, and belongs to the same document type.
- An existing active group of the same type is reused, and a member create never creates a group.
- An unknown group, an archived group, or a group of another document type is rejected with one validation outcome and writes no file.
- Group and member records use their own role field sequence, with independent order.
- A top-level field sequence on a `grouped-record` type is rejected.
- The title, field-option, required, clearing, `list`, `multiline`, and `--editor` rules of REQ-005.015 apply to both roles.
- The group link is the reserved front-matter `group` key, maintained only by the engine; no option sets or changes it.
- A member's group link cannot change after creation.
- Archiving or deleting a group changes no member's file, status, or identifier.
- A member is archived or deleted only by a command that names that member, and archiving a member preserves its identifier.
- A list shows group records and their members in group order, then member order, with member rows read-only.
