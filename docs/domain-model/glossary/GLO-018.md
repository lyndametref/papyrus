# GLO-018 Field Specification

## Definition
A declaration of one content field of a document type: a stable key, an optional body heading, a CLI option, a name and help text, a kind of text, multiline, or list, whether the field is required, and the verbs on which it may be set (a subset of create and edit). A field with a heading is stored in the body under that heading; a field without one is metadata-only and its key is its front-matter key. Its position in the field sequence is its order in the record body, in the command line, and in the browser form.

Also known as: FieldSpec, field.

Related terms: Document Type, Role, Record Model.
