# GLO-018 Field Specification

## Definition
A declaration of one content field of a document type: a stable key, at least one storage location (a body heading, a front-matter key, or both), a CLI option, a label and help text, a kind of text, multiline, or list, whether the field is required, and the verbs on which it may be set. Its position in the field sequence is its order in the record body, in the command line, and in the form.

Also known as: FieldSpec, field.

Related terms: Document Type, Role.
